# Annotations

Select any text in the rendered page and a small toolbar appears. Everything below was made with it, and it all lives inside this very Markdown file.

## Highlights in five colors

Mark the ==key sentence in yellow==, put <mark style="background:#c3edc0">supporting evidence in green</mark>, keep <mark style="background:#bcd8ff">definitions in blue</mark>, flag <mark style="background:#ffcfe1">open questions in pink</mark>, and <mark style="background:#ffd8a8">action items in orange</mark>.

中文一样好用：==这句话被高亮了==，而且高亮直接写进 .md 文件，换个编辑器打开还在。

## Formatting from the selection toolbar

You can make text **bold**, add <u>underlines for emphasis</u>, or ~~strike through what no longer applies~~.

## Notes as footnotes

Attach a thought to any passage[^n1], and it becomes a standard Markdown footnote[^n2] that works in Obsidian and on GitHub too.

## Review everything at once

Press Cmd+Shift+H to see every highlight and note across the whole folder, grouped by file. Your own private Readwise.

[^n1]: Notes are anchored to the exact text you selected.
[^n2]: Nothing is locked into the app; it is all plain Markdown.
