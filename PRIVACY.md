# Inkdown Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 25, 2026

Inkdown does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal data. Period.

- **No accounts.** The app has no sign-up, sign-in, or user identifiers.
- **No network use.** Rendering, search, translation, and AI features all run entirely on your device. The app makes no network requests.
- **Your files stay yours.** Markdown and PDF files you open are read from and written to your own storage (device or iCloud Drive) through Apple's standard file access. Their contents never leave your device through Inkdown.
- **No analytics, no tracking, no third-party SDKs.**
- **On-device intelligence.** Translation uses Apple's on-device Translation framework; Explain and Summarize use Apple's on-device Foundation Models. Both process text locally.

Settings such as theme, text size, recent files, and reading positions are stored locally on your device and can be removed by deleting the app.

Questions: open an issue at https://github.com/lytreallynb/markreader-ios or contact the developer via https://yutonglv.com.

# Inkdown 隐私政策

最后更新：2026 年 7 月 25 日

Inkdown 不收集、不存储、不传输、不共享任何个人数据。

- **没有账号体系**：无注册、登录，无任何用户标识。
- **不使用网络**：渲染、搜索、翻译与 AI 功能全部在设备端运行，应用不发起任何网络请求。
- **文件归你所有**：你打开的 Markdown 与 PDF 通过苹果标准文件访问机制读写于你自己的存储（设备或 iCloud 云盘），内容不会经由 Inkdown 离开你的设备。
- **无分析、无追踪、无第三方 SDK**。
- **端侧智能**：翻译使用苹果端侧 Translation 框架，解释与摘要使用苹果端侧 Foundation Models，均在本地处理。

主题、字号、最近文件与阅读位置等设置仅保存在设备本地，删除应用即清除。

如有疑问：在 https://github.com/lytreallynb/markreader-ios 提 issue，或经 https://yutonglv.com 联系开发者。
